04 — ProcessFrom first message to final files

Simple process. No surprises.

  1. 01

    Brief

    Tell us what you need in the two-minute Project Planner or on WhatsApp: goals, style, timeline and budget range.

  2. 02

    Fixed quote

    Moiz replies with a clear scope, a fixed price and a timeline in writing, before any work starts.

  3. 03

    Design & build

    You review the work at agreed checkpoints. Revision rounds are included in every package.

  4. 04

    Launch & handover

    Final files, a live website or ready-to-post videos, handed over with everything you need to use them.

07 — QuestionsStraight answers

Good to know.

How much does a project cost?
It depends on what you need. Each package on the Packages page lists exactly what is included. Send your brief through the Project Planner and Moiz replies with a clear, fixed quote before any work starts.
How long will my project take?
It depends on the scope and how quickly content and feedback come in. Your quote includes a timeline, and you can tell us your preferred deadline in the Project Planner.
Do you work with clients outside Karachi and Pakistan?
Yes. Mogify Studio is based in Karachi and works with businesses across Pakistan and worldwide. Everything runs smoothly over WhatsApp, email and video calls.
What do you need from me to get started?
A short brief is enough: what you need, your goals and any references you like. The Project Planner walks you through it in about two minutes and sends it straight to us.
How do revisions work?
Every package includes revision rounds, listed on the package. We share work at clear checkpoints so feedback is easy, and extra rounds are available as an add-on.
Can I get a custom package?
Yes. If your project mixes services (for example a logo, website and launch reel), pick them all in the Project Planner and we will put together one package for you.
Who will I be working with?
You talk directly with Muhammad Moiz Ali, the founder of Mogify Studio, from the first message to the final files. No account managers in between.
