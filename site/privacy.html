Privacy policy

Short and plain: what this website does and does not collect.

Last updated: 31 October 2026

This website belongs to Mogify Studio, Karachi, Pakistan. We keep it simple and collect as little as possible.

What this website collects

  • This website has no user accounts, no database and no advertising trackers.
  • The Project Planner saves your answers only in your own browser (local storage) so you do not lose progress. You can clear it any time with the “Start over” button or by clearing your browser data.
  • When you tap “Send on WhatsApp” or “Send by email”, your message opens in WhatsApp or your email app. Nothing is sent until you press send there.

Information you send us

If you message us on WhatsApp or by email, we use your details only to reply, prepare a quote and work on your project. We do not sell or share your details with anyone for marketing.

Third-party services

WhatsApp, email providers and social networks you choose to contact us through have their own privacy policies.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete your messages and project details at any time: email mogifystudio@gmail.com.
